Frequently Asked Questions

Will the alarm sound come from the speaker?

EarphoneNap is designed for earphone use. Actual behavior may be affected by device settings, connection status, iOS behavior, notification permissions, and volume settings.

What happens if my earphones disconnect?

Depending on your settings, the app may continue with vibration only or stop notifications. Detailed behavior may vary depending on the app version and settings.

Does it work in the background?

The app is designed to work during background use and while the device is locked. However, iOS changes, power-saving settings, device state, and notification permissions may affect behavior.

Does it support Live Activity?

Yes. Live Activity is supported. Display availability and appearance, including Dynamic Island, depend on the device and iOS version.

How can I restore my purchase?

The app is designed to restore Premium based on App Store purchase information. If restoration does not appear correctly, please check your Apple ID and network status.

How can I request a refund?

Refunds for App Store in-app purchases are handled through Apple's refund process. Developers cannot directly change Apple's payment or refund processing.
