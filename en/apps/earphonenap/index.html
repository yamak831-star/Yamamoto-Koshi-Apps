EarphoneNap

Alarm and timer app designed for earphone use

EarphoneNap is an alarm and timer app designed for short naps and breaks with earphones. It focuses on a quiet wake-up experience that is easier to use in shared spaces.

App overview

EarphoneNap is not a general-purpose alarm app. It specializes in helping you finish short naps and breaks comfortably, with quick setup for either a wake-up time or a timer duration.

Use cases

Lunch break naps, short rest during commutes, quiet wake-ups in cafes or shared spaces, power naps between work sessions, and quick break management.

Environment

EarphoneNap is an iPhone app that requires iOS 17.0 or later. Live Activity and Dynamic Island availability depend on the device and OS conditions.

Key features

Focused tools for managing short breaks with earphones.

Time settings

  • Alarm mode for a specified wake-up time
  • Timer mode for hours, minutes, and seconds
  • Snooze while the alarm is ringing
  • Pause, resume, and cancel controls

Notifications and display

  • Designed to play sound through connected earphones
  • Sound, vibration, volume, and fade-in options
  • Progress display through Live Activity and Dynamic Island
  • Japanese, English, and system-language display support

Earphone behavior

  • Monitors wired earphones, Bluetooth, USB Audio, and other connection states.
  • If earphones are not connected and sound is enabled, audio may not play.
  • If vibration is enabled, vibration can still notify you when sound does not play.
  • You can choose what happens when earphones disconnect during an alarm.
  • Sound does not automatically resume after reconnection once it has been disconnected.

User settings

  • Alarm time, timer duration, and snooze duration
  • Notification sound, vibration, volume, and fade-in
  • Earphone disconnection behavior
  • Earphone connection status display
  • Warning for unstable Bluetooth connections
  • App display language

Free version and Premium

The free version includes ads. Banner ads may be shown, and interstitial ads may appear after an alarm ends.

EarphoneNap Premium is a one-time non-consumable in-app purchase, not a subscription. Premium removes ads and unlocks additional sounds. Premium applies to the content available at the time of purchase and does not guarantee future feature additions, sound additions, specification changes, or updates.

Available sounds

  • Chime

Premium sound examples

  • Acoustic Guitar, Breeze, Flute, Harp
  • Kalimba, Music Box, Ukulele, Vibraphone, and more

Important notes

This app is designed for earphone use. Behavior may be affected by earphones, device status, iOS, Bluetooth, notifications, volume, power-saving settings, and other conditions. Do not use this app for important appointments or for situations where a failure could affect life, body, property, or other serious interests.
