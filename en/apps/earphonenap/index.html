EarphoneNap

Alarm and timer app designed for earphone use

EarphoneNap is an alarm and timer app designed for short naps and breaks with earphones. It focuses on a quiet wake-up experience that is easier to use in shared spaces.

App Overview

EarphoneNap is not a general-purpose multifunctional alarm app; it specializes in making short naps and breaks comfortable. You can quickly set "when to wake up" or "how long to nap," and with notifications designed for earphone use, it works seamlessly in offices, during commutes, cafes, and at home.

Problems This App Solves

  • You want to nap without disturbing others with speaker volume
  • You need to nap quietly in offices, during commutes, or at home
  • You want to quickly set your wake-up time or nap duration
  • You want to avoid missing notifications if your earphones fall out
  • You want to check remaining time while your app is in the background or screen is locked

Main Use Cases

  • Lunch break naps
  • Short breaks during commutes
  • Quiet wake-up in cafes and shared spaces
  • Power naps between work sessions
  • Managing short 30-minute breaks

Key Features

  • Supports both alarm and timer functions
  • Monitors earphone connection status for easy usage tracking
  • Notification design optimized for earphones makes it easy to use in quiet environments
  • Customize wake-up with sound, vibration, or fade-in options
  • Supports pause, resume, and cancel functions
  • View progress from lock screen or Dynamic Island using Live Activity
  • Multi-language support: Japanese, English, and system settings

Feature List

Time Settings

  • Alarm function: Ring at a specified time
  • Timer function: Set how many hours, minutes, and seconds until it rings
  • Snooze function: Extend the alarm by a specified number of minutes while ringing

Notifications

  • When earphones are connected, sound plays through the earphones
  • Vibration notification support
  • Volume control
  • Volume fade-in
  • Sound selection

Runtime Control

  • Clear running screen showing remaining time and progress
  • Supports pause, resume, and cancel operations
  • Maintains state when returning from background

Display and Integration

  • Live Activity support
  • Dynamic Island support
  • In-app language switching

Earphone Specifications

  • Monitors wired earphones, Bluetooth, USB Audio, and other connection states
  • When earphones are connected, sound plays through them
  • If earphones are not connected and sound is enabled, no audio will play
  • If vibration is enabled, you'll still receive vibration notifications
  • You can select "vibration only" as your notification method
  • Unstable Bluetooth connections can trigger a warning
  • You can choose what happens when earphones are disconnected during alarm
  • Options are "continue vibration only" or "stop all notifications"
  • Sound won't automatically resume if earphones are reconnected after being disconnected (safe design)

Free Version and Premium

Non-premium users see advertisements. The design includes banner ads and interstitial ads after alarms end.

Premium is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. Premium purchase removes all ads and unlocks additional sounds. Purchase restoration is supported.
